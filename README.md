Vermilion Harbor is an open-world crime game in a neon port city at dusk: five districts, ten story jobs, a five-star police force with roadblocks and Air One, a six-weapon wheel, a three-person crew with callable abilities, territory control with passive income, a four-tier garage with upgrades, street races with betting, three radio stations, day/night and rain, and a save system. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make GTA 6” bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to move and drive, E to enter a car, F to steal one, mouse to aim, click to shoot, Q for the weapon wheel, 7/8/9 to call your crew, R for radio, P to pause, S to save.
